Wild Willows is a nature-restoration sim. You're the caretaker of a preserve that's seen better days: you gather materials, craft and plant real habitat, reshape the terrain, and animals return as each biome recovers. The ecology is the actual game: species only come back when the habitat can support them, and predators won't settle until there's prey to feed them.

No failing, no clock, no combat. Just a quiet preserve slowly coming back under your care.

How it works

Start in a damaged meadow

You start with a tent, a campfire and an empty basket on stripped ground. Gather what's fallen, plant what belongs, dig out the water. The same patch fills back in, and the animals return on their own.

Six biomes to restore

Each biome tracks its own Health and Balance and has its own restoration goal: replant the meadow's grasses and wildflowers, rebuild the forest's understory, refill the wetland. Restoring one opens the trail to the next.

Willow MeadowOld Hollow ForestRushwater WetlandRedstone ScrublandGraywind HeightsPelican Shore

Craft where you stand

334 recipes, built straight from your basket and chests: 254 habitat pieces (alder snags, basking logs, dragonfly ponds, duck nest boxes), 66 plants that grow in over time, 39 things to make for your home, and 30 structures and paths. No workbench. No menus inside menus.

A living, fact-checked food web

All 150 species are real, with their real diets, shelters, and habitat needs, researched from the Cornell Lab of Ornithology, the National Park Service, US Fish & Wildlife, Audubon, the Smithsonian, the IUCN Red List, NOAA, and more. That research drives the simulation: herbivores need the right plants standing, predators only settle once there's prey to feed them, and each biome tracks the balance of its whole food web. The field guide fills in as animals return, and it reads like a journal, not a textbook.

Good to know

What is Wild Willows?

A cozy nature-restoration life sim. You set up camp at the edge of a damaged nature preserve, then gather materials, craft and plant real habitat, and shape the land. As each biome recovers, real animal species find their way home.

Is Wild Willows free?

There's a free demo you can play right in your browser at play.wildwillows.app. The full game is a one-time purchase on the Mac App Store and on itch.io. No ads, no in-app purchases, no loot boxes.

What platforms is it on?

The full game is on the Mac App Store for macOS, and on itch.io for macOS, Windows, and Linux. The free browser demo is at play.wildwillows.app. Nothing to download.

What languages does it support?

English and Spanish. You can switch language on the title screen or in Settings at any time.

Does it have accessibility options?

A lot of them, all in one Settings screen and all changeable mid-game: dark mode (or follow your system), high contrast, three colorblind modes, five interface fonts, four text sizes, reduce motion, a low graphics-quality mode for older machines, simpler wording, an interaction highlight, fully rebindable keys, and separate music and sound-effect toggles with their own volumes. The full list is in Made to be played by more people.

Is there any combat or a way to lose?

No. There's no combat, no enemies, and no way to fail. Animals are observed and welcomed home, never hunted, captured, or lost, and nothing ever dies.

Can I use Wild Willows in a classroom?

Yes, and there’s a kit for it: a free educator guide and six student worksheets, both up in For teachers. The lesson runs in one 45–60 minute period for grades 5–8 and works with the free demo. If cost is what’s standing in the way, I keep a limited number of free promo codes for classrooms. Tell me a bit about why you want to use it with your class and write to wildwillowsgame@gmail.com.

Is it family-friendly?

Yes. It's gentle, non-violent and educational, and rated 9+ on the App Store.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game is fully playable offline, with no account and no sign-in. Your world lives on your own device.
